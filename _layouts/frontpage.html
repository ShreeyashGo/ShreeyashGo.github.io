Shreeyash Gowaikar

Hi there! I'm a Computer Science master's student at Georgia Tech, specializing in Machine Learning. I work with Prof. Kai Wang in the AI for Social Impact Team.


My research has taken me from responsible AI at UdeM to agentic systems and code intelligence at Microsoft Research, and applied research on self-healing production agents at DoorDash. Across these settings, a recurring challenge has been making AI dependable in practice.


Those experiences shaped my interest in AI robustness and safety. Today, I focus on fine-tuning robustness, bringing diffusion models and LLMs together to understand how adaptation affects model behavior and how to preserve safety and reliability.
