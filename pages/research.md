  <h1>AI Robustness and Reliability</h1>
  <p class="teaser">Building dependable AI for applications that benefit people and society.</p>
  <p>My research spans conversational AI, agentic systems, language-model reasoning, and responsible AI. Through work at Microsoft Research, DoorDash, and UdeM, I’ve been drawn to building AI that addresses practical problems and has meaningful societal impact.</p>
  <p>The variability of language and multimodal models raises questions about how dependably they can support real-world tasks. These questions have shaped my growing interest in AI robustness and safety, and motivate my current research on making model behavior more reliable.</p>
  <h2 class="font-size-h3">Research interests</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Conversational and agentic AI:</strong> dialogue, reasoning, and agents that help people with real-world tasks.</li>
    <li><strong>AI robustness and safety:</strong> understanding failure modes and improving the reliability of language and multimodal models.</li>
    <li><strong>AI for social impact:</strong> applications that benefit people, with attention to fairness and the communities they affect.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>For concrete examples, explore <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/publications/#cosmir">COSMIR’s structured memory for long-context reasoning</a> and <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/publications/#preference-fairness">fairness in preference learning</a>. For applied research, see my <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/aboutMe/#experience">DoorDash and Microsoft Research experience</a>.</p>
